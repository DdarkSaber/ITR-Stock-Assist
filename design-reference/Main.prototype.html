WHICH REPORTS
  • Groww: Stocks Capital Gains Report and Mutual Funds Capital Gains Report, downloaded as .xlsx
  • Zerodha: the Tax P&L capital-gains report. Your app needs a Zerodha reader for this; it isn't built yet
  • Any broker: your checked lots in the app's CSV template

Not accepted: P&L statements, PDFs, old .xls files.

SEVERAL ACCOUNTS?

Add every account's report and tag it. The ITR has no per-account rows, so the gains are added together into one return. You still get a per-account breakdown to check against each broker, and duplicate sales across files are flagged.
