STEP 1 / UPLOAD

Drop in your broker reports.

Add the capital-gains report from every account you used this year. Files are read in this browser tab only.

Only validated financial years are offered.

No files selected. Data stays in memory until you reset or close this tab.

The first review downloads the public Python runtime; XLSX import/export loads the runtime's package installer and bundled openpyxl wheels. Internet is needed for initial loading. No analytics, account, local storage or financial-data requests.

WHICH REPORTS
  • Groww stocks: the Stocks Capital Gains Report for the year, downloaded as .xlsx.
  • Groww mutual funds: the Mutual Funds Capital Gains Report, downloaded as .xlsx.
  • Any other broker: your verified lots in the canonical CSV template, one disposed acquisition lot per row.

Not accepted: P&L reports (realised trades overlap and unrealised holdings are not disposals), PDFs, old .xls files.

Download canonical sample CSV
SEVERAL ACCOUNTS?

Add every account's report together. The ITR has no per-account rows, so gains are combined into one return. You still get a per-account breakdown to check against each broker, and duplicate lots across files are flagged.

BROKER FIGURES, WITHOUT ASSUMPTIONS

Native reports show gross gains and provisional broker totals. Missing expense, STT and cost-history confirmations stay pending, not invented. The synthetic sample includes one intentionally blocked lot.

    A working paper, not a tax return.
