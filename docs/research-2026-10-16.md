# ITR-2 research and implementation decisions

Reviewed **16 October 2026**. Scope: Indian listed delivery equity and verified equity-oriented MF disposal lots; FY 2024-25 / AY 2025-26 and FY 2025-26 / AY 2026-27. This is a bounded public-source review, not an exhaustive survey, legal opinion or certification of a complete return. No private files, identities, financial values or project source were submitted to research services.

## Source hierarchy and decisions

Official AY-specific schemas/validation rules establish **field names and reporting structure**, not proof that a transaction qualifies for a tax section. Official legislation is primary for legal treatment; CBDT explanations provide context. Broker/RTA material establishes report semantics, not tax law. Blogs/forums identify usability problems; they cannot authorize tax calculations.

All URLs below were accessed/attempted on the review date. Dates distinguish publication, modification and release-directory metadata. No proposed bill was treated as enacted law.

## Legal and access limitations

Direct current-year statutory text for Income-tax Act 1961 sections **2(42A), 48, 111A, 112A, 55(2)(ac)** was sought through [official Acts](https://incometaxindia.gov.in/Pages/acts/income-tax-act.aspx) and section/tutorial endpoints. Several returned 403/HTML rather than usable law. A Finance Bill PDF is a proposal, not proof of enactment, and was not used to implement a rule. We did **not** independently reverify every statutory proviso, exception or notification in this pass; no new tax rate, STT exception, grandfathering formula, set-off or exemption calculation was added.

Existing eligibility checks remain deliberately conservative: canonical `stt_paid=yes` attests relevant conditions; the app's equity-share acquisition+transfer wording is conservative for this MVP, **not a claim that section 111A always requires acquisition STT**. Acquisition exceptions need advice rather than automatic admission. A Groww row is never promoted to attested status by this research. Corporate-action/buyback/bonus/dividend-stripping issues can require information absent from reports.

Targeted Reddit searches and publicly accessible Reddit/old.reddit pages did not yield a verifiable relevant thread (access/login responses). **No Reddit post is claimed as reviewed or used.** Search-generated summaries sometimes mixed dates, AYs, placeholder codes and even STT deduction claims. Those summaries are not evidence. We did not bypass access controls, use authenticated/private discussions or claim “all forums” coverage.

## Deliberate non-implementation

- No final Schedule CG/112A/SI amount, annual exemption allocation, current/prior-year loss set-off or carry-forward; no tax liability. Net working gains are not taxable income. Current-year losses, brought-forward losses and exemption interactions need full-return inputs.
- No Schedule CG Table F / `AccruOrRecOfCG` figures. Raw dates are preserved, but final period allocation after loss adjustments is not known.
- No grandfathering, FIFO, acquisition-history reconstruction, fee guessing, invented STT, residency/DTAA/FII branches or official upload files.
- No debt/international/hybrid/unverified ETFs, business income, intraday or F&O calculations; unsupported lots remain visible and excluded. No AIS integration.
- No combined foreign-assets/stocks filing guide, Windows packaging, deployment or changes to the existing foreign-assets project.

## Delivered improvements

`stock_assist/filing.py` adds year-aware candidate destinations, observed-only groups separated by evidence basis, gross-positive/negative-loss/net columns and a documented field-evidence table. Workbook opens on a formatted **Filing review**, followed by **Filing groups**, **Filing map** and **References**, with the original five evidence sheets retained. Blank means unknown; final values are not invented. Browser review uses the same grouped model. See [workbook contract](workbook-contract.md) for future-reader safeguards and cross-project alignment recommendations.
